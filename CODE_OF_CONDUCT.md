# Code of Conduct

CSyk follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space, and to anyone
representing the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, accept that people disagree, and leave out harassment,
insults and personal attacks.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers at
[luphihung111@gmail.com](mailto:luphihung111@gmail.com). Every report is reviewed and kept
confidential. The maintainers decide on consequences as the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe.
